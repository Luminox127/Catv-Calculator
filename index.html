About these calculations

Reference values were transcribed from the supplied app screenshots at 55, 250, 550, and 1000 MHz. At these frequencies, the results match the screenshots for 100 feet.

Between those frequencies, attenuation is estimated by linear interpolation. Total cable loss = loss per 100 feet × length ÷ 100. Supported range: 55–1000 MHz. Actual attenuation varies by cable manufacturer, construction, and temperature. Connector and splitter losses are not included.

This recreation uses the reference app’s appearance and sample values. The original app’s full calculation method was not provided.
